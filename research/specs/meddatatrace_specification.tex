\section*{Executive summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive summary}
Medical-AI papers often depend on public datasets whose releases, partitions, labels, or documentation later receive corrections or issue reports. A dataset-level warning does not establish that a particular experiment is affected: the experiment may use a corrected release, a custom partition, an unrelated component, or only cite the dataset. Conversely, a renamed or derived artifact can preserve a relevant issue even when the dataset name changes.

\textbf{MedDataTrace is designed to automate reconstruction of that experiment-specific provenance question from public scientific artifacts.} For a named dataset issue and a named evaluation, the system retrieves and structures documented data use, resolves releases/splits/components where the evidence permits, aligns the recovered facts to the issue's scope, and applies a deterministic issue contract. The output is a scoped state such as \status{APPLIES}, \status{NOT\_APPLICABLE}, \status{UNKNOWN}, or \status{CONFLICTING}, with explicit evidence locators and limitations.

The scientific contribution under investigation is not a new graph database, ontology, LLM pipeline, or generic issue-status pattern. Close work already covers dataset tracking, living reviews, structured medical-AI metadata, lineage reconstruction, and cross-domain applicability statements \cite{inthepicture2025,sourget2024,atlas,tracing2026,openvex}. The credible contribution prospect is an artifact-grounded benchmark and ML pipeline for reconstructing experiment-level dataset provenance---especially the linkage from a particular reported evaluation to the exact versioned artifacts it used---and determining the applicability of known, mechanically testable dataset issues under incomplete, versioned, and conflicting public evidence.

The primary validation deliberately avoids unavailable expert adjudication. Cases enter the primary gold benchmark only when the complete reference derivation---reported evaluation, source-supported artifact linkage, identity/membership basis, and deterministic issue-specific result---can be reproduced from frozen public artifacts. Weak supervision and LLM-assisted labels may be used as \emph{silver} training data, but not as gold ground truth. Cases that require domain judgment beyond the frozen contract remain visible as \status{REQUIRES\_EXPERT\_REVIEW}; cases whose reference derivation cannot be established are \status{REFERENCE\_UNSCORABLE}. Both are excluded from gold accuracy claims. A reproducibly justified \status{UNKNOWN} is a distinct gold state, not a synonym for an unscorable reference.

The project begins with one concrete historical issue in the DermaMNIST/HAM10000 family. A six-study micro-pilot measures public-evidence recoverability, artifact-grounded reference feasibility, recurring extraction tasks, and real implementation effort. A broader graph platform, GraphRAG, supervised fine-tuning, or future expert user study is conditional on evidence that the task is recoverable and technically useful.

\begin{decisionbox}[title=Current commitment]
Build and evaluate the artifact-grounded evidence-reconstruction task before building a platform. The project can succeed as a reproducible benchmark and ML provenance-reconstruction study even if expert user evaluation, a hosted graph application, or GraphRAG is never justified.
\end{decisionbox}

\section{Project definition}
\label{sec:project}
\subsection{Problem statement}
A paper can cite a dataset without using it experimentally. An experiment can use a renamed derivative, a corrected release, a custom split, or only an associated annotation tool. A later issue report may concern only one component or one historical partition. Dataset tracking in medical imaging is already complicated by inconsistent citation and naming practices \cite{sourget2024,copycats}.

The project addresses the resulting \emph{review task}. It does not assume that every dependency is problematic or that every documented problem propagates unchanged. Its core output is a qualified relation between a specific evaluation and a documented issue, supported by inspectable evidence.

\subsection{Overall goal}
\textbf{Overall goal:} automatically reconstruct enough experiment-level dataset provenance from public scientific evidence to determine whether a frozen, artifact-verifiable dataset issue applies to a reported medical-AI evaluation, while preserving uncertainty instead of inventing missing facts.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{13mm}Y Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Objective} & \textbf{Observable result}\\ \midrule
O1 & Recover experiment-specific data dependencies. & Structured records distinguish dataset family, release, split, component, transformation, and usage role.\\
O2 & Assess issue applicability with explicit scope and uncertainty. & Every assessment identifies proposition, claim target, evidence basis, and unresolved questions.\\
O3 & Preserve scientific boundaries. & Dependency, applicability, measured effect, and clinical consequence are never collapsed.\\
O4 & Create a reproducible benchmark without expert-dependent gold labels. & Primary test cases have reproducible evaluation-to-artifact derivations and deterministic reference states; silver, reference-unscorable, and expert-required cases are labeled separately.\\
O5 & Establish a genuine ML task. & Rules, direct LLM extraction, and learned/embedding-based extraction are compared on held-out studies and end-to-end applicability.\\
O6 & Retain graph relevance without making it the novelty claim. & Graph context/projection is evaluated only where it improves resolution, retrieval, or maintainability.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Primary user and decision}
The first target user is a researcher reviewing an existing medical-imaging evaluation. The primary decision is whether a named, documented dataset concern warrants a scoped qualification or recheck, whether a specific missing artifact should be requested, or whether no action is justified \emph{for that concern}. Benchmark maintainers and experiment designers are secondary users.

Direct clinical use, patient advice, model certification, misconduct allegations, and universal model-trust scores are outside scope. MedMNIST itself states that its benchmark is not intended for clinical use \cite{medmnist}. The intended benefit is better research review, not demonstrated patient benefit.

\subsection{Four levels that must remain separate}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{32mm}Y L{30mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Level} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Project position}\\ \midrule
Documented dependency & An evaluation or linked development run consumed an identified data component. & In scope.\\
Issue applicability & The consumed component and setup satisfy the documented issue's conditions. & In scope, with abstention.\\
Measured effect & The issue changed a metric, ranking, or model behavior. & Out of scope without separate empirical reanalysis.\\
Clinical consequence & The issue changed real-world decisions or outcomes. & Out of scope.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection{Primary hypothesis}
\textbf{H1 -- automated evidence reconstruction.} On artifact-verifiable held-out cases, a structured evidence-reconstruction pipeline can recover experiment-level provenance and produce correct scoped applicability states with better evidence fidelity and/or fewer unsupported decisive outputs than simple alias/rule extraction and direct LLM assessment under the same public-source access, \emph{at a useful level of decisive coverage}. Error and coverage must be interpreted jointly; an always-\status{UNKNOWN} method is an explicit sanity baseline and cannot satisfy H1 merely by avoiding unsupported decisive outputs.

\textbf{H2 -- graph/context value (secondary).} Citation and lineage context may improve difficult entity/version/split resolution or evidence retrieval relative to text-only extraction. This hypothesis is optional and is tested only if the pilot reveals enough ambiguous cases.

These are hypotheses to test, not promises. The first study is descriptive. A later confirmatory study would require a larger artifact-grounded test set, frozen model choices, and prespecified margins informed by pilot results.

\section{Contribution and prior-art position}
\label{sec:prior}
\subsection{What is not claimed as novel}
The project does not claim novelty for: tracking datasets used in papers; a knowledge graph of papers, models, and datasets; LLM extraction of structured metadata; scientific entity/relation extraction from full text; weak supervision for scientific mention detection; dataset lineage reconstruction; generic issue propagation; a status vocabulary; or GraphRAG over medical literature. Relevant prior work already covers substantial parts of that space \cite{inthepicture2025,sourget2024,atlas,tracing2026,copycats,openvex,scirex2020,scier2024,scidmt2024}. The stronger distinction pursued here is linking a \emph{particular reported evaluation} to versioned artifacts whose issue-specific implications can then be checked under an explicit evidence boundary.

\subsection{Contribution hypothesis}
The strongest originality prospect is:

\begin{decisionbox}
\textbf{An evidence-qualified method and benchmark for assessing whether a documented dataset issue applies to a specific medical-imaging evaluation, with explicit treatment of release/split scope, corrections, contradictory evidence, identity limitations, and missing information.}
\end{decisionbox}

A claim of methodological advantage requires evidence that the proposed pipeline improves provenance recovery, evidence fidelity, abstention behavior, end-to-end applicability accuracy, or implementation effort relative to strong alternatives. A benchmark or empirical case-study contribution does not require the method to win; it requires new, reliable, reusable knowledge about which experiment-level provenance facts are publicly recoverable, which documentation gaps block assessment, and what automation can or cannot resolve. A null comparative result is informative only when it is produced by a rigorous, reusable study rather than by implementation failure.

\subsection{Capability comparison obligations}

\section{Pilot domain, public data, and sampling}
\label{sec:data}
\subsection{Initial domain}
The first family is HAM10000 / DermaMNIST. A 2025 investigation reports historical DermaMNIST partition overlap, additional duplicate-identity problems, and corrected artifacts \cite{abhishek2025,zenodo}. This provides a real issue anchor. It does \emph{not} establish that downstream papers disclose enough information to determine applicability.

The pilot is intentionally narrow. Generalization beyond this family requires additional separately documented issue mechanisms or dataset families.

\subsection{Public data sources}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{38mm}Y Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Source type} & \textbf{Use} & \textbf{Constraint}\\ \midrule
Peer-reviewed/open literature & Methods, data-use statements, corrections, reported evaluation setup. & Redistribution and text-mining rights remain source-specific.\\
PMC Open Access Subset \cite{pmcoa} & Reusable full text for eligible articles. & Accessibility does not imply identical reuse rights; use supported retrieval services.\\
Dataset documentation / archives & Release identity, partitions, corrections, metadata, provenance. & A public page is not proof that all underlying data can be redistributed.\\
Project-created records & Artifact-derived facts, issue contracts, evidence locators, silver labels, and mechanically generated reference states. & Must preserve source licenses and avoid redistributing restricted material.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

No patient-level graph is required. Raw patient images or restricted clinical data are not a project dependency.

\subsection{Naturalistic and enriched collections}
\textbf{Naturalistic collection.} Freeze discovery queries, aliases, dates, eligibility rules, and selection order before inspecting applicability. Retain inaccessible, unresolved, and unparseable cases in the appropriate denominators. This estimates recoverability within the declared frame.

\textbf{Enriched challenge collection.} Deliberately seek corrections, conflicts, mixed inputs, transformed artifacts, and identity limitations. This tests reasoning coverage and cannot estimate real-world prevalence.

Synthetic fixtures are a third collection used only for conformance testing.

\subsection{Reference tiers: gold, silver, and unscorable}
The primary benchmark uses evidence tiers rather than expert adjudication.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{28mm}Y Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Tier} & \textbf{Admission rule} & \textbf{Permitted use}\\ \midrule
Artifact-grounded gold & The complete derivation is reproducible from frozen public artifacts: the named evaluation is source-supportedly linked to the exact artifact/membership basis, identity assumptions are explicit, and the issue-specific reference state is deterministically computed. No domain-specific judgment beyond the contract is required. & Primary test/evaluation set. Reference derivation, generator, inputs, hashes, assumptions, and rule version are released where rights permit.\\
Silver & Labels are proposed by high-precision heuristics, structured metadata, LLM extraction, or weak supervision but are not mechanically sufficient for gold admission. & Training/development, candidate discovery, error analysis. Never reported as gold accuracy.\\
Reference-unscorable / expert-required & The benchmark builder cannot establish a reproducible reference because evaluation-to-artifact linkage, identity, admissible evidence, or domain interpretation remains unresolved. & Retained in coverage denominators and qualitative failure analysis; excluded from gold correctness claims. \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

A case is not upgraded to gold because an LLM is confident. If the \emph{complete reference derivation}---including the evaluation-to-artifact link and identity assumptions---cannot be reproduced from the frozen artifact bundle and issue-specific reference generator, it is not a primary gold case. A reproducibly justified \status{UNKNOWN} may be gold; an unresolved reference construction is not.

\section{Evidence model and assessment semantics}
\label{sec:evidence}
\subsection{Define the proposition first}
Each assessment concerns a proposition
\[
Q(e,i,s,b,t):\quad \text{issue } i \text{ is present in evaluation } e
\text{ within scope } s,\text{ boundary } b,\text{ snapshot } t.
\]
The issue definition names the affected component/version, grouping unit, relevant use relation, claim target, and sufficient evidence for presence or exclusion.

\textbf{Claim target is mandatory.} Evidence may concern a \status{REPORTED SETUP}, a \status{DOCUMENTED PROCEDURE}, or an \status{EXECUTION-LINKED ARTIFACT}. These are not interchangeable. Static splitting code can support a procedure-level claim without proving which artifacts produced the reported result.

\subsection{Evidence-state table}
For an exactly scoped proposition $Q$, let $p$ mean admissible evidence supports $Q$ and $n$ mean admissible evidence supports its negation. Neither flag is created by model confidence or by failure to find contrary evidence.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ccL{38mm}L{61mm}@{}}
\toprule
$p$ & $n$ & \textbf{State} & \textbf{Meaning}\\ \midrule
0 & 0 & UNKNOWN & Neither conclusion is sufficiently supported.\\
1 & 0 & APPLIES & Positive applicability evidence for this scope.\\
0 & 1 & NOT\_APPLICABLE & Positive evidence excludes the issue for this scope.\\
1 & 1 & CONFLICTING & Material evidence supports both positions for the same proposition.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{MITIGATED} is a reason for a supported negative result when a documented correction is shown to be implemented and sufficient for the named scope. It is not a separate truth value.

\subsection{No negative-by-default rule}
Absence of a sentence saying ``we corrected the split'' is not evidence that no correction occurred. Conversely, a precisely documented unchanged use can support a conclusion about the \emph{reported setup} without proving the absence of every undisclosed action. Conclusions must stay within their evidence target.

\subsection{Completeness boundaries}
An inventory can be based on documented inputs, an author-declared inventory, or an execution-linked manifest. A complete assessment of documented inputs does not establish that undisclosed inputs did not exist. A positive witness can support a localized warning even when other inputs remain unresolved; a whole-boundary negative result requires adequate coverage of all relevant scopes for that proposition.

\subsection{Multi-scope composition contract}
Assess the smallest meaningful scopes first, then summarize them using an ordered policy. The summary is a reporting rule over already scoped assessments; it does not strengthen their evidence.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{12mm}Y Y@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Order} & \textbf{Condition} & \textbf{Evaluation-level summary and qualification}\\ \midrule
1 & At least one conflict-free APPLIES scope. & APPLIES \emph{to the named positive scopes}; preserve every UNKNOWN/CONFLICTING scope and any incomplete inventory. Do not imply that all inputs are affected.\\
2 & No conflict-free positive scope, but at least one CONFLICTING scope. & CONFLICTING; identify the scopes and the conclusion blocked by the conflict.\\
3 & No positive/conflict; at least one UNKNOWN scope, incomplete inventory, or no assessed scopes without independent proof that the relevant inventory is empty. & UNKNOWN; identify unresolved scopes, missing coverage, and the bounded next check.\\
4 & Every relevant scope has a supported negative result and coverage is complete relative to the named boundary. & NOT\_APPLICABLE within that boundary; preserve whether each negative arose from exclusion or a supported correction.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

A conflicted scope cannot serve as an unqualified positive witness. A clean positive on one input plus a conflict or unknown elsewhere yields a localized warning with residual uncertainty. A whole-boundary negative result requires complete coverage of the scopes relevant to that boundary, but it does \emph{not} reintroduce a requirement to reconstruct unrelated memberships: DM-H01 N1 remains satisfied by any supported evidence that blocks every frozen historical witness as specified in Section~\ref{sec:dmh01}.

\subsection{Method inputs versus reference artifacts}
The evaluated method may receive the public source corpus, attributed source statements it extracts, literal version names, source-local roles, evidence locators, and the frozen issue definition. It must not receive the withheld mechanically generated reference state or the private mapping file used solely to score the benchmark.

The primary reference is mechanically reproducible \emph{under explicit, source-supported artifact-linkage and identity assumptions}. The reference record must expose the complete derivation from the named evaluation to the exact artifact/membership basis, the source locator that supports that linkage, the identity mapping used, every non-mechanical transcription or preparation step, and the deterministic issue computation. Hashing a manifest proves which bytes were used; it does not by itself prove that the manifest corresponds to the reported evaluation.

Human handling is permitted when it is transparent and non-adjudicative---for example, transcribing an explicit evaluation identifier, recording a source locator, or checking a deterministic join. The evaluated extractor's own uncertain mapping must never be reused to generate its gold answer. Cross-document identity resolution, mapping a source mention to the relevant artifact, retrieving the required evidence, and producing the final applicability state remain tasks for the evaluated pipeline.

If a conclusion depends on medical/domain interpretation outside the frozen contract, the case is marked \status{REQUIRES\_EXPERT\_REVIEW} or unresolved. Such cases remain evidence about coverage limits but are excluded from primary gold correctness metrics.

\section{First issue-specific contract: DM-H01}
\label{sec:dmh01}
\subsection{Scientific proposition}
The first issue contract concerns persistence of documented historical overlap witnesses. Let $W_0$ be a pinned, reproducibly generated or source-enumerated set of historical oriented image-pair witnesses, each with partition identity, evidence, metadata version, identity limitations, and artifact hash where available. When the source relationship is symmetric (for example, two images documented as representing the same underlying lesion for purposes of the historical split concern), the registry stores \emph{both} orientations $(a,b)$ and $(b,a)$. It must not manufacture unreviewed transitive links. A deliberately directional issue would require a different issue contract.

For a selected evaluation pair $s$, let $D_{e,s}$ and $T_{e,s}$ denote the final development and test memberships at the chosen claim target.

\[
Q_{H01}(e,s)\equiv \exists(x,y)\in W_0:\; x\in D_{e,s}\land y\in T_{e,s}.
\]

This proposition asks whether at least one \emph{documented historical witness} survives into the named development--test use pair. Storing both orientations prevents a symmetric witness from being treated as excluded merely because the two endpoints exchange development/test roles. The proposition does not mean ``all lesion leakage'', ``all train/test overlap'', or ``the model is invalid.''

\subsection{Sufficient evidence routes}
\begin{longtable}{@{}L{20mm}L{69mm}L{62mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{Sufficient evidential basis} & \textbf{Important limit}\\ \midrule
\endfirsthead
\toprule\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{Sufficient evidential basis} & \textbf{Important limit}\\ \midrule\endhead
P1: witness presence & An identified $W_0$ witness has supported membership on the development and test sides of the linked final use pair, matched to the evaluation and claim target. & Same dataset family, same split ratio, or ancestral use without final membership is insufficient.\\
P2: reported inheritance & The paper explicitly reports unchanged use of exactly identified historical partitions, and the anchor evidence establishes a witness in that exact partition pair. & ``Official split'', an unspecified subset, or a citation alone is insufficient.\\
N1: witness exclusion & For every oriented witness in frozen $W_0$, supported evidence establishes absence of at least one required membership under validated identity mapping and the named target. & Missing membership is not absence; checking only some witnesses cannot support exclusion.\\
N2: correction & The assessed evaluation uses an exactly identified correction, and a reproducibly checked postcondition establishes exclusion of $W_0$ for the relevant scope. & A correction's existence, citation, or ``cleaned'' wording alone is insufficient.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\subsection{N1 does not require unnecessary reconstruction}
N1 requires complete \emph{witness coverage}, not necessarily complete reconstruction of every unrelated member of both input sets:
\[
\forall(x,y)\in W_0:\quad \operatorname{supported}(x\notin D_{e,s})\;\lor\;\operatorname{supported}(y\notin T_{e,s}).
\]

Complete development and test manifests are one sufficient route. A complete test manifest that excludes every relevant test-side witness endpoint can also suffice. Mixed per-witness exclusion evidence is permitted. Each absence must be supported under the named snapshot, target, and identity mapping; an incomplete manifest or unrecognized alias cannot establish absence.

A negative DM-H01 result excludes only this frozen historical witness set. It does not establish general biological separation, patient separation, or lack of other overlap.

\subsection{Conflict and identity handling}
An assertion that all covered use pairs are non-overlapping must be compared with a supported witness in any contained pair when issue, target, snapshot, and identity basis align. Different scope identifiers do not prevent a contradiction when one proposition logically contains the other.

Recorded identifiers are not automatically biological identity. The dermatology investigation reports missed duplicates assigned different lesion identifiers \cite{abhishek2025}. Therefore a non-overlap check on recorded lesion IDs supports only an identifier-level statement unless stronger identity evidence exists.

\section{Benchmark construction and reference generation}
\label{sec:benchmark}
\subsection{Reference layers}
Keep four layers separate:
\begin{enumerate}
\item raw public sources and immutable artifact pointers;
\item structured source-local facts produced by deterministic parsing or extraction candidates;
\item withheld artifact-grounded reference states produced by the reference generator;
\item silver labels and reference-unscorable/expert-required cases, which must never be silently promoted to gold.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reference derivation as a provenance chain}
For every proposed gold case, construct a withheld derivation record of the form
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{reported evaluation} &\rightarrow \text{source-supported artifact link}\\
&\rightarrow \text{identity/membership basis}\\
&\rightarrow \text{issue computation} \rightarrow \text{reference state}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The reference generator must not skip the middle of this chain. An artifact hash authenticates the chosen file but does not establish why that file belongs to the named evaluation. The record therefore includes: evaluation locator; linkage source/locator; literal identifiers or configuration references; artifact/version hash; identity mapping/version; preparation/transcription steps; deterministic computation inputs; and final scoped state. This derivation is withheld from evaluated methods but released with the benchmark where rights permit.

\subsection{Artifact-grounded gold admission}
A case is eligible for the primary gold set only when the \emph{entire reference derivation} is inspectable and reproducible. All of the following must hold:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the issue contract is frozen and sufficiently specific for the claim target;
\item the reported evaluation is identified by a stable study/evaluation key and source locator;
\item a source-supported linkage establishes which exact artifact, configuration, manifest, or membership basis corresponds to that evaluation and claim target; if several plausible artifacts remain and the linkage cannot be resolved, the case is reference-unscorable rather than gold;
\item the relevant public artifacts can be identified and hashed or otherwise version-pinned;
\item the identity mapping and its limitations are explicit and versioned;
\item the reference proposition can be evaluated by deterministic code from explicit memberships/identifiers/structured evidence or an equivalently machine-checkable source condition;
\item every non-mechanical preparation step needed to construct the reference is disclosed, and reproducing the final state does not require a new medical or clinical judgment;
\item the reference generator and all non-restricted inputs needed to reproduce the state are versioned.
\end{enumerate}

A source sentence such as ``we used the official test split'' may support a reported-setup linkage, but it is not by itself a gold label. Gold status arises only when that source statement is connected to the frozen artifact basis, identity assumptions, and issue contract through an inspectable derivation. Native configuration that explicitly names the evaluation and exact manifest is stronger linkage evidence than an association inferred from similar names; either route must state its assumptions rather than hiding them in a structured file.

\subsection{Silver data and weak supervision}
Training/development data may be built from high-precision patterns, structured repository/configuration metadata, citation heuristics, or LLM-proposed structured extraction. These records are explicitly labeled \status{SILVER}. Spot checks may be used for engineering quality control, but no medical-expert adjudication is required for the core benchmark and silver data are not used as the primary held-out ground truth.

If enough artifact-grounded slot labels can be derived automatically, component tasks may additionally report role/version/split extraction metrics. Otherwise the principal correctness claim remains end-to-end applicability on artifact-grounded gold cases.

\subsection{Gold uncertainty versus reference-construction uncertainty}
Two forms of uncertainty must remain separate.

\textbf{Assessment uncertainty} is a legitimate reference outcome when the frozen admissible evidence channel reproducibly establishes that neither polarity is justified. Such a case may enter gold with reference state \status{UNKNOWN}. For example, a uniquely identified execution manifest may explicitly omit a contract-required field, so the evidential incompleteness itself is reproducible. Failure of a parser or search procedure to find an informative sentence does \emph{not} establish gold \status{UNKNOWN}.

\textbf{Reference-construction uncertainty} means the benchmark builder cannot determine a reliable reference state---for example, because two manifests plausibly correspond to the result and no source-supported linkage resolves which one was used. This case is \status{REFERENCE\_UNSCORABLE}, not gold \status{UNKNOWN}. A domain-dependent case is \status{REQUIRES\_EXPERT\_REVIEW}. Both remain in coverage/failure accounting but outside correctness metrics.

Gold \status{CONFLICTING} likewise requires a reproducible admission route: materially opposed admissible evidence must address the same proposition, target, scope, and snapshot. If the benchmark cannot establish whether the sources are actually comparable, the case is reference-unscorable rather than gold \status{CONFLICTING}.

Do not force a label when the complete derivation cannot be established. Record the excluded case, the unresolved linkage/fact, what artifact would resolve it, and whether the limitation arose from access, identity resolution, missing execution linkage, incomparable evidence, or domain judgment.

\subsection{Class-specific gold admission}
Gold states are admitted only through demonstrated reference routes:
\begin{itemize}
\item \status{APPLIES}: a fully linked artifact basis deterministically satisfies the positive issue contract.
\item \status{NOT\_APPLICABLE}: a fully linked artifact basis deterministically satisfies the negative/exclusion contract for the named scope and boundary.
\item \status{UNKNOWN}: the frozen admissible evidence channel itself reproducibly lacks a contract-required fact, so neither polarity is justified. Retrieval failure by the evaluated method or by the benchmark builder is not sufficient.
\item \status{CONFLICTING}: reproducible admissible evidence supports both polarities for the same proposition, target, scope, and snapshot.
\end{itemize}
If no real case qualifies for one of these routes, that class is absent from the gold test and is reported as such.

\subsection{Diversity and holdouts}
Report studies, evaluation setups, issue mechanisms, corrections, dataset families, and reasoning patterns separately. Many cases derived from one historical issue do not constitute many independent issue mechanisms.

Hold out all versions of a study together. Maintain a study-level \emph{development-validation} collection for prompt design, thresholds, model choice, error analysis, and ablations. Freeze a separate final gold test set before those choices are finalized, and do not inspect final-test labels or outputs until the pipeline and decision rules are frozen. Early feasibility cases used to refine rules are development material and cannot later be described as untouched tests. Where real artifacts permit, include compositional challenge cases involving transformation plus role, partial correction across multiple inputs, or contradictory evidence.

\section{Comparative evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}
\subsection{Evaluation tracks}
\textbf{C -- conformance.} Synthetic certificate inputs exercise the deterministic evidence-state and multi-scope composition contract. This validates bookkeeping only.

\textbf{A -- artifact-grounded assessment.} Methods receive the same frozen structured/public artifact bundle needed to resolve the issue but not the withheld reference state. This isolates entity/scope alignment and deterministic assessment from full-text retrieval noise.

\textbf{E -- end-to-end source-to-assessment.} Methods receive the same raw permitted paper/document/repository corpus and comparable retrieval/model budgets. They must recover dataset usage, role, release/split/component identity, evidence locators, and the final scoped state. This is the primary ML evaluation.

\textbf{S -- silver extraction development.} Weakly supervised or LLM-proposed labels may be used for training, ablation, and error analysis. Results on S are not substituted for artifact-grounded gold evaluation.

\subsection{Primary scoring unit}
The canonical technical atom is
\[
a=(e,i_{version},u_{dev},u_{test},target,snapshot).
\]
The primary held-out outcome is the exact scoped applicability state for each artifact-grounded gold atom, together with a valid evidence path/locator set where the contract requires one. Because status is many-to-one with respect to provenance, final-state correctness is \emph{not sufficient}: where the gold derivation exposes these slots, score the evaluation-to-artifact link, development/test roles, exact release/split or configuration identifiers, identity basis, and whether each cited locator actually supports the asserted use rather than merely mentioning the artifact.

For scientific scoring, semantically identical repeated answers for the same atom are collapsed to one candidate answer and duplication is reported separately. Repetition never earns additional true positives. Incompatible answers for one atom remain invalid. Same-status outputs with materially different evidence or scope are not automatically merged.

\subsection{Accuracy, abstention, and evidence measures}
Report exact status accuracy and a confusion matrix only across gold states for which a real admission route is represented in the frozen test set. If there are no gold \status{UNKNOWN} or \status{CONFLICTING} cases, report that absence rather than implying four-class evaluation. For reference-applicable cases report warning precision and applicable-case recall. Interpret unsupported-decision error jointly with decisive coverage (or compare methods at matched decisive coverage); abstention on an applicable case counts against recall.

Keep gold correctness and non-gold verification outcomes in separate denominators. On a reference-unscorable case, a decisive output is counted as a contract/evidence failure only when its submitted evidence can itself be mechanically shown to violate the frozen contract. If the output simply cannot be verified by the available reference procedure, mark it \status{UNVERIFIED}; do not silently convert ``not verified'' into ``wrong.''

\begin{align*}
\mathrm{Precision}_A &= \frac{\#\text{correct scoped APPLIES outputs}}{\#\text{all scoped APPLIES outputs}},\\
\mathrm{Recall}_A &= \frac{\#\text{correct scoped APPLIES outputs}}{\#\text{gold APPLIES cases}}.
\end{align*}

Also report evidence-locator fidelity, evaluation-to-artifact linkage accuracy, entity/release/split resolution success where artifact-derived slot labels exist, role accuracy, identity-basis accuracy, supporting-locator precision (a locator must establish the asserted use, not merely mention the artifact), unsupported decisive-output rate, decisive coverage, actionable-UNKNOWN rate, gold-eligibility/coverage rate, reference-unscorable rate, parser/source failures, runtime/cost, counts, and uncertainty. Small samples remain descriptive. Grouped studies and repeated issue instances must not be treated as independent Bernoulli trials merely to tighten intervals \cite{nistbinomial}.

\subsection{ML error analysis}
Every end-to-end error should be assigned, when possible, to a failure stage: dataset mention/use classification, role extraction, entity resolution, evaluation-to-artifact linkage, version/split resolution, correction linkage, evidence retrieval, scope alignment, deterministic contract logic, or output serialization. This decomposition is an important analysis contribution because it shows where the tested learned methods add value, where rules suffice, and where public evidence remains inadequate; categorizing errors alone does not prove that a learned method is required.

\section{Usefulness and cost}
\label{sec:usefulness}
\subsection{What usefulness can be claimed in the solo study}
The core study does not claim that clinicians, reviewers, or researchers make better decisions because no expert user study is required. Its directly testable usefulness is narrower: automatically recover experiment-level provenance that would otherwise require manual source chasing; distinguish artifact-verifiable applicability from blanket dataset-level warnings; expose the exact missing artifact when a conclusion cannot be made; and quantify how much of the task is publicly recoverable.

A downstream user-facing value claim therefore remains a hypothesis until external users are studied. The project may still be useful as research infrastructure if it provides reproducible evidence records, a benchmark, and reliable automated provenance reconstruction.

\subsection{UNKNOWN should be actionable}
An UNKNOWN report names the missing artifact or fact, explains why it would resolve the proposition, records whether it was found in searched sources, gives a bounded next check, and provides a fallback when the artifact is unavailable. ``Inspect the manifest'' is not actionable if no manifest can be located.

\subsection{Effort and automation accounting}
Measure the effort that the solo project can actually observe: source discovery time, artifact/version resolution time, scripting/curation time, model inference cost, graph/retrieval cost, and manual engineering spot-check time. Do not imply domain-expert time savings that were not measured.

For a declared common cost unit and $r$ comparable reuses,
\[
\Delta C(r)=\Delta C_0+\Delta M+r\,\Delta c.
\]
This model can be used to estimate when one-time curation becomes worthwhile, but any claimed human-workflow saving must be labeled hypothetical unless independently measured.

\subsection{Optional future external validation}
If domain experts or suitable research users later become available, a reader study may be added as a separate validation layer. It is not a prerequisite for the benchmark, ML experiments, or project completion. Any such future study must separate reference construction from reader evaluation, prevent prior exposure to the same cases across conditions, and report preparation costs as well as reader time.

\subsection{Why a graph is useful, but replaceable}
The graph naturally represents recursive lineage, component-level derivation, experiment-specific roles, and downstream issue navigation. It is particularly useful for queries that follow multiple transformations and then return to experiments. However, recursive SQL or adjacency lists can represent the same facts. The project should demonstrate graph value through maintainability, query expressiveness, or workflow quality, not claim automatic accuracy gains from Neo4j.

\subsection{ML responsibilities}
ML is central to the automatic track. The target transformation is
\[
\text{raw scientific artifacts}\rightarrow\text{structured experiment provenance}\rightarrow\text{deterministic issue assessment}.
\]
The ML layer may include: dataset mention/use classification; training/validation/testing/pretraining role extraction; canonical dataset/entity resolution; release/version/split/component resolution; correction and transformation linkage; and retrieval/ranking of evidence passages. Begin with deterministic aliases/rules, add a direct constrained-LLM baseline, then evaluate learned biomedical/scientific text models or embedding/reranking approaches only where the pilot reveals enough examples and a measurable baseline gap.

Silver labels may support training. Prompt design, thresholds, architecture/model choice, and ablations use study-level development-validation data only. A separate final artifact-grounded test set remains untouched until those choices and the assessment policy are frozen. Model confidence never creates a scientific fact; low-confidence or unresolved extraction remains explicit.

Graph context may be used as an ML feature or retrieval constraint when citations, aliases, derived datasets, or lineage relations help resolve entities. GNN/link prediction is optional and, if tested, may only propose candidate relations for verification rather than insert unsupported provenance as fact.

GraphRAG is optional and explanation-only: it may answer ``why is this case flagged?'' by retrieving the accepted evidence path. It cannot create new scientific facts or override the deterministic assessment.

Use references 41--64 identified in the primary DermaMNIST investigation as the bounded initial candidate frame; they are examples selected by the issue authors, not a representative sample of medical AI \cite{abhishek2025}. Assign each unique study a stable key (normalized DOI, otherwise arXiv root ID, otherwise normalized title/year). Order by a frozen deterministic hash and inspect the first six. Do not replace inaccessible, citation-only, unresolvable, or expert-required studies to manufacture gold cases.

Before generating primary gold labels: pin and hash or otherwise version-identify the DM-H01 witness/artifact basis; verify that the reference generator reproduces the intended witness-persistence proposition from those public artifacts; complete at least one full evaluation-to-artifact reference derivation and reproduce it from a clean checkout/environment using the released instructions; record every source-supported linkage, identity qualification, transformation policy, and non-mechanical preparation step; freeze the candidate list and selection rule; freeze the evidence-search budget and gold-admission criteria, including class-specific routes for gold \status{UNKNOWN}/\status{CONFLICTING}. \subsection{What the micro-pilot can and cannot establish}
Six studies calibrate workload, gold-reference feasibility, public-evidence coverage, and recurring automation targets. They cannot establish general effectiveness, prevalence, clinical validity, or broad originality. A useful result may be that version/split resolution is repeatedly difficult and worth automating; another may be that most cases are simple enough for rules; another may be that the chosen issue cannot support artifact-grounded gold evaluation at all. Each outcome directly informs whether MedDataTrace should grow, simplify, or pivot.

These tests validate bookkeeping assumptions only. They do not determine whether a real passage satisfies a scientific evidence obligation, validate medical evidence, or demonstrate usefulness.
